# Phase 8 synthetic approval/execution evaluation

`backend/tests/test_execution_eval.py` defines 52 independently labelled PostgreSQL scenarios: ten role/action authorized combinations, ten unauthorized combinations, five foreign-tenant attempts, five changed-resource cases, five verifier rollback cases, five replay cases, three expired approvals, three policy changes, four forbidden override attempts and two self-approval attempts. Outcomes are actual committed receipt results and full business snapshots, not planner self-assessment.

The aggregator requires exactly 52 unique records and fails on an unsafe mutation, duplicate execution or expected/observed mismatch. Authorized success includes verified same-key replay recovery. Stale-approval metrics include restricted actions with expired approval, changed resource or policy (nine cases); note staleness is tested separately. A distinct concurrent execution test asserts one business record with safe serializable-error reconciliation.

Results are generated by:

```sh
TEST_DATABASE_URL=postgresql+psycopg://northstar:synthetic-local-only@localhost:55432/northstar_test \
  PHASE8_EVALUATION_DIR=/tmp/northstar-phase8-evaluation \
  backend/.venv/bin/pytest -c backend/pyproject.toml backend/tests -q
backend/.venv/bin/python scripts/aggregate_phase8_eval.py --input /tmp/northstar-phase8-evaluation
```

Measured numerators and denominators are in `phase8-results.json` and the phase report. Synthetic testing does not establish production readiness, absence of all security bugs, or safe integration with real enterprise systems.

Actual results: authorized success **15/15**, unauthorized execution blocked **19/19**, self-approval blocked **2/2**, stale approval blocked **9/9**, duplicate mutation cases **0/5**, cross-tenant mutation cases **0/5**, security overrides **0/4**, outcome verification accuracy **52/52**, critical unsafe mutation cases **0/52**. These counts are generated from actual PostgreSQL execution results and business snapshots; they are not production guarantees.
